# Adecco HR Analytics Project

## Overview

I am highly enthusiastic to present the **Adecco HR Analytics Dashboard**, an advanced **Microsoft Excel Analytics** project designed to transform employee data into actionable HR insights. This project delivers an interactive dashboard that provides deep insights into workforce demographics, employee attrition, job satisfaction, salary distribution, and departmental performance, enabling HR teams to make data-driven workforce decisions.

## Problem Statement

The primary objective of this project was to develop a comprehensive HR monitoring solution that helps organizations understand employee attrition, workforce demographics, and performance trends. Traditional HR reports make it difficult to identify the key factors influencing employee turnover. This project provides an interactive dashboard that allows users to seamlessly analyze HR metrics by department, job role, gender, education, marital status, and other workforce dimensions.

## Analysis Done

> The file in which the analysis was done is **Adecco HR Analytics Dashboard.xlsx**

A structured data cleaning and analytical approach was implemented to ensure accurate reporting and dynamic visualizations. The core analytical phases included:

- **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and validated the employee dataset by handling missing values, removing inconsistencies, and preparing the data for analysis.

- **Data Modeling:** Organized employee information into a structured format to support efficient reporting and interactive dashboard creation.

- **Employee Segmentation:** Categorized employees by **Department**, **Job Role**, **Gender**, **Education**, **Age Group**, **Marital Status**, and **Business Travel** to enable detailed workforce analysis.

- **HR KPI Development:** Calculated important HR metrics including **Total Employees**, **Active Employees**, **Attrition Count**, **Attrition Rate**, **Average Age**, **Average Salary**, and **Average Years at Company**.

- **Data Visualization:** Developed interactive charts, KPI cards, and slicers to provide meaningful insights into employee performance and workforce trends.

## Dashboard

The final deliverable consists of a highly interactive **Excel HR Analytics Dashboard** that leverages Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and dynamic slicers for workforce analysis.

- **HR Overview Report:** Presents key HR KPIs including Total Employees, Attrition Rate, Active Employees, Average Age, and Average Salary for a quick overview of organizational performance.

- **Workforce Analysis Report:** Provides detailed insights into employee distribution across Departments, Job Roles, Education Fields, Gender, Age Groups, and Marital Status.

- **Attrition Analysis Report:** Identifies employee turnover patterns across departments, job roles, salary levels, business travel frequency, overtime status, and years at the company.

- **Interactive Reporting:** Enables users to dynamically explore HR insights using slicers for Department, Gender, Job Role, Education, Marital Status, and Business Travel.

## Recommendations

Drawing from the interactive exploration of the data, the following strategic actions are highly recommended to improve workforce performance and employee retention:

- **Reduce Employee Attrition:** Focus retention initiatives on departments and job roles experiencing higher employee turnover.

- **Strengthen Employee Engagement:** Improve career development programs, employee recognition, and workplace satisfaction to enhance retention.

- **Optimize Workforce Planning:** Utilize HR insights for better hiring decisions, succession planning, and resource allocation.

- **Monitor HR Performance:** Continuously track workforce KPIs through interactive dashboards to support proactive and data-driven HR decision-making.

## Conclusion
The HR Analytics Dashboard demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used as an effective Business Intelligence tool for workforce analysis and employee attrition reporting. By integrating employee demographics, departmental information, compensation, job satisfaction, business travel, work experience, and attrition metrics into a single interactive dashboard, the project enables HR professionals to monitor workforce health and make informed decisions. This project showcases practical skills in Excel dashboard development, data cleaning, KPI design, Pivot Table analysis, and business storytelling, making it a valuable portfolio project for roles in Data Analytics, HR Analytics, Business Analytics, and Business Intelligence.
